Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026

No data collected

Now Departing does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app has no accounts, analytics, or advertising.

Location

Your device's location is used only on your device, to find the subway stations near you. It is never stored or shared.

Your favorites

Favorites are saved only on your device, where the widgets can read them. If you use the Apple Watch app, they sync directly between your iPhone and your watch.

Live Activities

The countdown on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island is updated by the app on your device. No push server is involved.

Network requests

Arrival times and service alerts come directly from the MTA's real-time data feeds, and the list of stations is downloaded from the app's GitHub repository. These requests never include your location. Get Directions opens Apple Maps, subject to Apple's privacy policy. No intermediary server is involved.

This website

This site uses no cookies or analytics. The home and game pages load a pixel font from Google Fonts.
